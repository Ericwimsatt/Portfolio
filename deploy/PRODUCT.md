Primary users are hiring managers and founders evaluating Eric Wimsatt for engineering roles, collaborations, or early product-building opportunities. ## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to make Eric legible as a product engineer with a frontend craft bend. It should communicate practical engineering judgment through clear work examples, but the frontend craft should be shown through the quality of the site itself rather than explained as a claim.

## Positioning

The portfolio should present Eric as an engineer who can bridge product thinking, implementation, and human-feeling interface craft: future-oriented without becoming cold, technical without losing warmth, and visually distinctive without distracting from the work.

## Operating Context

Visitors will evaluate the site in hiring, founder, and collaboration contexts. The core browsing pattern is fast credibility scanning followed by deeper inspection of selected experience, projects, and links. The site should support concise first-pass reading and reward slower exploration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The portfolio will showcase experience at Roblox and UC Berkeley, plus selected GitHub projects. Specific project list, role details, dates, case-study depth, repository links, and any private/confidential constraints remain open decisions.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed evidence categories: Roblox experience, UC Berkeley experience, and GitHub projects. No specific testimonials, metrics, screenshots, logos, project names, or repository URLs have been provided yet, so future work must not fabricate those claims.

## Product Principles

- Show craft through the artifact before naming it.
- Make credibility scannable for busy hiring and founder audiences.
- Balance future-facing technology with human warmth and natural texture.
- Use real experience and project evidence; label placeholders clearly until replaced.
- Let visual distinctiveness support trust rather than perform around it.
